# Editorial review for courses

The writing should make a difficult idea easier to understand and use. Review
the full assigned copy, including titles, examples, captions, questions, hints,
feedback and project instructions. Keep technical substance while removing
padding and performed cleverness. Read for repeated observations as well as
missing explanations.

## Use your editorial guidance in the appropriate register

When you supply an editorial protocol, style guide or voice profile, read its
current guidance and only the explicitly permitted exemplars needed for the
target register. Keep private corpus text, interviews and unpublished material
out of course assets and portable reports.

The checks below cover editorial lenses, grounding, antecedents and edit
provenance. They need no external style repository or separate editing panel.
When no profile is available, use these checks and say no profile was loaded.

Use the course's teaching voice. A request to de-slop a course doesn't call for
imitating someone's personal essays. Prefer plain, direct explanations with
natural variation. Preserve good humor and distinctive wording that fits the
lesson. Don't insert pet phrases, self-deprecating asides, catchy triads,
rhetorical questions or invented experiences to satisfy a personality quota.
Current user preferences and applicable register rules take priority over older
essay habits. Voice preferences aren't universal grammar rules.

## Review through each lens

One editor can run these as separate passes. Split long assignments by lesson
with a shared contract, then have the lead reconcile transitions and terminology.
Do not equate six lenses with six mandatory model calls.

| Lens | What to examine | Useful finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developmental | Course goal, sequence, section purpose, example placement, repetition and missing steps | Identify what understanding is lost if a section is removed, or the bridge needed before a hard exercise. |
| Line | Clear subjects and verbs, connections between sentences, abstraction, unnecessary qualifiers, rhythm | Quote a passage and propose a clearer explanation with the same conditions and causal meaning. |
| Copy | Terms, quantities, grammar, labels, references, code/prose agreement and instructions | Name the exact inconsistency and affected action. Preserve API names and valid code. |
| Voice and AI phrasing | Hype, slogans, contrived contrasts, repetitive symmetry, over-formal phrasing and fake casualness | Replace a decorative claim with the concrete mechanism, or cut it if the previous sentence already did the work. |
| Credibility | Unsupported facts, invented anecdotes, overstated outcomes and missing qualifications | Keep simulation, measured execution, hypotheses and results distinct. Cite support or identify a gap. |
| Reader value | What the reader can explain, predict, decide or build after the passage | Test the instructions using only context the course supplies, identifying the missing scene, example or consequence. |

For every section, check whether a newcomer to this course has met the objects
and events it refers to. An event known only from repository history or a prior
chat hasn't been introduced to the reader. Supply a small grounded example when
authorized, or identify the missing information. A glossary cannot replace
a missing explanation of what happened and why it matters.

Look for summaries that restate the introduction, headings that promise more
than the section delivers, and paragraphs that could fit any AI course unchanged.
Preserve examples, counterexamples and repeated practice when they serve distinct
learning purposes. Cut repeated conclusions before compressing the useful work.

## Mechanical scan followed by judgment

Where possible, extract learner-facing text into a local review file with a map
back to the unit, component or rendered state. Include alternate feedback and
hints. Separate prose from code, metadata and quoted examples so scan counts
aren't inflated by source syntax or deliberate examples of bad writing.

If your project provides a prose scanner, inspect its documented interface and
run its read-only checks on the extracted text. Save its output beside the
evidence. Without one, inspect equivalent patterns with available local text
tools and disclose the reduced mechanical coverage. No scanner is required.
Do not copy a private tool into a public skill bundle.

Review candidates in context:

- Hype, grand promises, empty encouragement and stock introductory filler.
- Maxim-like titles, dramatic reveal setups and clever closing lines that repeat
  the preceding point. Prefer a title that names the thing being learned.
- Contrived “not X, but Y” reframing, matching sentence openings, stacked triads
  and a run of short disconnected claims. Restore the logical connection.
- Repeated fingerprint words such as “genuinely,” “quietly,” “simply” and
  “actually.” Count within the relevant unit, not one frequency cap for a book.
- Repeated bold lead-ins, unnecessary sections, ornamental punctuation and
  stiff wording. Preserve useful lists, stressed negation and exact notation.
- Unintroduced tools/events, assertions without mechanisms and fabricated
  first-person experience. Those need grounding, not a smoother sentence.

A match is a review candidate, not an automatic defect. A real contrast may be
essential to the concept. Repeated technical terminology may improve precision.
Explain why an edit helps the reader. Don't chase a zero-hit score, a word-count
target or an AI-detector score. A scan doesn't verify clarity, truth or authorship.

## Propose exact edits and protect meaning

For each substantive edit return the location, exact old text, proposed new text,
the reader's problem, and the reason the change helps. Add source/claim references
where needed. Distinguish must-fix errors from improvements and taste preferences.
Mark a pure addition as an addition. Group repeated instances only when all their
locations remain recorded. Do not rewrite the learner's own assessment response.

Check each proposed replacement against the original and the source:

- Same quantities, units, conditions, exceptions and scope of claim.
- Same distinction between example, simulation, actual execution and evidence.
- No removed prerequisite or step needed to reproduce the exercise.
- No hint that gives away a protected answer before the intended commitment.
- No change to code, expected results or grading hidden inside a prose edit.

Route a meaning change to teaching/assessment review. Concision doesn't justify
turning “passed these finite checks” into “is correct for all inputs.” An additive
fix should supply a needed explanation or grounded example, not extra personality.
Unsupported personal material remains a named gap. Routine course editing can
continue using neutral exposition without requiring an author interview.

In audit mode, retain proposed edits without applying them. If editing is
authorized, preserve the original, apply agreed fixes, rerun the scanner and
re-read the rendered text. Recheck affected claims, examples and assessment paths.
The final adversarial pass asks whether the revision introduced an overclaim,
contradiction, missing context or unusable instruction, and records what survived.
Keep the recheck bounded and avoid repeated panels without a new question.

Record whether text was agent-written, user-directed or typed by the user.
Approved agent wording remains agent wording. If you later request
voice learning, freeze the actual AI version before human edits and preserve the
real pair under your chosen protocol. This QA skill doesn't update a personal voice
profile, exemplars or memory, or claim that synthetic edits teach personal voice.

## Calibrate the editor

The lead supplies a small pair without expected labels. Include fluent prose
that overclaims from a weak test and a precise technical explanation that should
be kept. A proposed edit must both improve the weak passage and preserve the
good one's conditions. Add a misleadingly short rewrite that drops a required
denominator, exception or uncertainty. Reject it despite its smoother wording.

Keep detections, missed issues and false positives. Preserve at least one passage
that didn't need editing, with a reason. Report exact coverage, supported edits,
scanner counts/limitations and meaning checks. This demonstrates a bounded review,
not universal de-slopping quality or fidelity to an author's voice.
